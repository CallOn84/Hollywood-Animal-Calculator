        What this assumes

        • The writer's skill is left out. Art and commercial points are the flat amount your tags add, the same for every writer.
        • Some rules that only apply in a full playthrough are left out: Tags the game marks as trash. Any one of them makes the script a trash movie, which scores much lower.         What Quick Mode does

        Quick Mode is a planning tool. You pick the story elements of a movie script, and it shows how the game will score them before you commit to it in-game.

        How to use it

        Choose at least one genre, a setting and a protagonist, then press Calculate. Everything else is optional. After that the results update as you change things, and your choices are saved on this device.

        What you get

        • Script compatibility says how well your tags fit together, from 1 to 5. A score of 3 is neutral.
        • Score modifier says how much that fit raises or lowers the script's base quality.
        • Art appeal and Commercial appeal are the points your tags add to each side.
        • Leans towards shows which side is stronger.

        Good to know

        The numbers come from the game's own data. The tool leaves out the writer's skill and a few rules that only apply in a full playthrough. The details are under "What this assumes" in the results.
